Summit Evolution Mechanics

• Central Floating Mark: Reticle is fixed at the center of the display.

• Roaming Imagery: Left Stick pans the terrain smoothly beneath the central mark.

• Z-Index Image Parallax: Right Stick shifts the images relative to each other until the rooftop fuses at the mark with zero eye strain!

VR Headset Controls

  • Left Stick Roam Terrain (X, Y) beneath mark
  • Right Stick Up/Down Shift Image Parallax (Z) to fuse surface
  • Right Stick Left/Right Zoom In / Out
  • Right Trigger Digitize 3D Vertex
  • Left Grip Reposition Station in 3D
  • Left Button X Undo | Y Redo
  • Right Button A Cycle Tool | B Finish Feature
